Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Artixon ("we", "us", "our") is a small, one-person business creating product card designs and e-commerce visual content. This Privacy Policy applies to the website at https://artur020202.github.io/artixon-site/ (the "Site") and to Artixon's use of the Pinterest API, and explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide directly to Artixon, such as your name and email address, when you contact us through the Site or directly.
  • Project content you share with us, such as product photos, brand assets and descriptions, used solely to complete design work you request.

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or any other tracking technologies on this Site, and we do not automatically collect browsing or usage data from visitors.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver requested design services.
  • To communicate with you about a project or request you've made.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Pinterest integration

Artixon's Pinterest integration is for our own account only.

We use the official Pinterest API exclusively to publish our own original content — images, descriptions, and links to Artixon's work and services — to Artixon's own Pinterest Business account. This integration is not offered to, or used by, clients or any other users; it is a single-account, first-party publishing tool.

We use n8n, a workflow automation tool, only to schedule and publish content that we have already created and approved ourselves. It does not create content automatically, act on other accounts, or interact with other users.

  • We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to other Pinterest users.
  • We do not access private data belonging to other Pinterest accounts.
  • We do not scrape data from Pinterest.
  • We do not send automated messages to Pinterest users or engage in automated outreach of any kind.

The integration is designed to request only the Pinterest account permissions necessary for the described publishing workflow, and access can be reviewed or revoked at any time through Pinterest's own account settings.

4. Data retention

We retain project files and contact information only as long as needed to complete a project and maintain reasonable business records, after which it is deleted or anonymized upon request.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you've shared with us. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

6. Third-party services

We use third-party platforms — Fiverr, Upwork, the official Pinterest API, and n8n for our own workflow automation — to communicate with clients and to publish our own content as described above. Each platform has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at arturo-portugalyano@mail.ru.
