What I do

Design that helps your product sell itself.

Every service below is built for one job: making a shopper stop, understand and buy — on a marketplace listing, a Pinterest pin or a paid ad.

01

Product Cards

Main and secondary listing images for marketplaces and storefronts — designed to pass platform review and stop the scroll in a crowded feed.

What you get
  • Main image + 4–8 secondary slides
  • Sized correctly per platform
  • Source files (PSD / Figma)
  • 1–2 revision rounds
02

E-commerce Infographics

Feature call-outs, size charts, "what's in the box" and comparison graphics that answer a shopper's questions before they have to ask.

What you get
  • Icon-based feature breakdowns
  • Size / spec comparison charts
  • Consistent visual language across a full catalog
03

Ad Creative & Pins

Static banners and pins sized correctly for paid social, display ads and Pinterest — built to earn the click without misrepresenting the product.

What you get
  • Platform-correct dimensions
  • Multiple headline / offer variants
  • Static formats (no video)
04

Brand Consistency Systems

A reusable visual system — layout grid, color rules, type and iconography — so every future card, ad and pin looks like it belongs to the same brand.

What you get
  • A short style reference (colors, type, layout rules)
  • Templates you or your team can reuse
  • Applied across your existing catalog
Process

How a project runs

STEP 1

Brief

You share product photos, references and what the listing needs to communicate.

STEP 2

Concept

You get a first layout direction before full production begins.

STEP 3

Design

Full set produced to platform specs, with your feedback folded in.

STEP 4

Delivery

Final files plus source, ready to upload.

Good to know

Which platforms do you design for?

Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, eBay, Pinterest and general paid social — each with its own image and safe-zone requirements.

Do you need my product in hand?

No — I work from the photos, specs and brand assets you provide.

How do we start a project?

Reach out through Fiverr, Upwork or email with your product and what you need — I'll scope it from there.
